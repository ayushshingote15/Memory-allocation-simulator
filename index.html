How the Q-learning demo works

The simulator trains a small Q-learning strategy selector on generated memory workloads. Its possible actions are First Fit, Best Fit and Worst Fit. The reward favours successful allocations and applies a small penalty to unsuccessful requests. The learned policy is then tested on the same input as the traditional algorithms. This is an educational demonstration, not a production operating-system memory manager; its results are not guaranteed to be better.
